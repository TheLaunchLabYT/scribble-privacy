Scribble Privacy Policy

Last updated: 09/10/2026 >

Scribble is a personal planner app for Android. This policy explains what personal data Scribble uses, why, who receives it, and what your choices are.

1. Who is responsible

Scribble is provided by Adam Friedlander, based in the Netherlands ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal data described here.

Contact for any privacy question or request: TheLaunchLabYT@gmail.com

2. The short version

  • Your tasks, timetable, activities, training plan, settings and journal are stored on your phone.
  • Your journal never leaves your phone.
  • An account is optional. You only need one to use the AI features.
  • When you use an AI feature, the planning data it needs is sent to our server and to Google's Gemini to produce the answer. We do not store your questions or the answers.
  • The Free version shows ads from Google AdMob. In Europe you are asked for your ad privacy choice first.
  • We do not sell your data.

4. What stays on your phone

Your tasks, schedule, school timetable, tests, activities, training plan, settings and journal are stored on your device. We cannot see them, except for the planning data sent when you use an AI feature (section 3). Your journal is never sent anywhere. If you uninstall Scribble or use "Delete everything" in settings, this data is erased from your phone. Backups you export are files you control.

5. Ads

The Free version shows one small ad on the home, tasks and journal pages, and a video ad only when you choose to watch one to earn an extra AI request. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. We do not share your tasks, journal or other planner content with advertisers.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you are asked for your ad privacy choice before ads are requested. You can change it at any time in Scribble under settings > data & privacy > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

6. Who receives your data

  • Google (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC): Firebase Authentication (accounts), Cloud Firestore (usage counter and reward records, stored in the EU), Gemini API (AI answers), AdMob and the User Messaging Platform (ads and consent).
  • Render (Render Services, Inc., United States): hosts the Scribble server that checks your sign-in, applies the daily allowance, and passes AI requests to Gemini.

These companies process data on our behalf or, for advertising, partly as controllers in their own right. Some of this processing takes place outside the European Economic Area, in particular in the United States. Such transfers rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified, or on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account: until you delete it.
  • AI usage counter: 90 days.
  • Rewarded-ad records: 13 months.
  • AI request content: not stored by us. Google processes it under its Gemini API terms.
  • Technical logs: kept by our hosting provider for a short period, normally no longer than 30 days.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its use, to receive it in a portable form, and to withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect what was done before.

  • Delete your account in Scribble under settings > account. To have the usage and reward records linked to it removed as well, email us.
  • Change your ad choices under settings > data & privacy.
  • For anything else, email [CONTACT EMAIL]. We reply within one month.

You may also complain to your data protection authority. In the Netherlands this is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).

9. Age

Scribble is intended for people aged 12 and over. If you are under 16 and live in the Netherlands, you need a parent's or guardian's permission to create an account or to consent to personalised ads. If you believe a child has given us data without that permission, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. The AI key, the reward-signing key and the database credentials exist only on our server, never in the app. The app cannot read or write the usage database directly. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit what we collect so that there is little to lose.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. For significant changes we will also tell you in the app.
